## 1. Purpose

Validate span verification beyond Experiment 028 smoke on a larger panel with multiple draft lengths, compressors, and verification modes.

## 2. Why this follows Experiment 028

Experiment 028 proved span ≡ sequential on 32 smoke cells. Experiment 029 extends to stratified V10 prompts × draft_len {2,4,8} before Phase 3 timing.
